House rules (rulings made by this app)
  1. A turn is: your cards age one round, then you either skip, or draw and take exactly one action — play one card, use one card, or discard one card (from hand or desk).
  2. Discarding from hand never triggers a card's effect (per rules Example 1, where discarding a play card earns nothing). Weapons and helpers must be used to take effect; unwanted ones can simply be tossed.
  3. A drawn Alert resolves immediately and uses up your whole turn. Fired! and Recession immediately discard affected desk cards with normal settlement, so earned hours bank and unrecovered costs remain lost. Alerts are never dealt in opening hands, and never appear in refill/redraw draws — only on your own turn's draw.
  4. Negative cards (Risky, Stock Market, Downsizing): the full cost is paid the moment the card enters play. One hour returns to you each round until repaid, then the card earns from the pile. Discarding while hours are still owed forfeits them. Expiring loses everything on the card and charges the base cost again.
  5. Risky jumps to a total of +8 on its 8th round (per its card text), then expires on the 9th.
  6. Professional pays +10 only when discarded on round 8 or later, up to its (possibly extended) deadline. Expiring pays nothing.
  7. Tardy ages the target card one round with no earnings — it can force an immediate expiration.
  8. Parasite moves 1 hour per round from its holder to its attacker, 4 hours total. Discarding it early pays the attacker the remaining balance at once.
  9. Quit: the attacker picks a visible played card, or "hand" (random card). A card discarded by Quit is voided — hours on it are lost, no costs charged, no effects. A player who lost a hand card refills to 5 free at the start of their next turn.
  10. Foreign Exchange swaps one random hand card from each side.
  11. Excused can be used from hand at any time, for free, to cancel a weapon aimed at you (never an Alert). As your action, it can also discard one unprotected weapon already on your desk without triggering that weapon's remaining effect. Against Downsizing it deflects: you are skipped and it moves on. Downsizing dies once every player has suffered or deflected it, and it charges its full cost to each new holder.
  12. Making room: if a play-weapon lands on a full desk, you discard one of your own cards normally (banking rules apply — this one isn't voided).
  13. While a weapon sits on your desk, your unprotected non-weapon cards neither earn nor recover owed hours. They still age and can expire. A card protected by Extension or Nepotism overrides this freeze.
  14. Performance Review: weapons in your hand are executed (you pick targets); play and helper cards discard with no effect; then you redraw to 5.
  15. Amnesia rebuilds every card in play as freshly played: earned hours return to the pile, attachments (Extension, Nepotism) are removed, and negative cards re-charge whatever you had recovered.
  16. Extension adds 5 rounds and blocks weapons from affecting its protected card. Nepotism also blocks weapons and stops normal expiry. Only Alerts bypass helpers.
  17. Sharing is Caring earns whenever its linked player's cards earn from the pile. It voids (hours lost) the moment any card of yours or theirs expires.
  18. Hour totals can go negative (tracked as debt, per rules Example 3). Scammer can't steal from a player at 0 or below.
  19. Game end: after the final round, cards still in play settle as if discarded — earned hours bank, unrecovered costs stay lost. Most hours wins; ties share the win.
